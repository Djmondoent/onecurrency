Getting Started

Set up your wallet, choose a domain, and finish the first backup.

Quick Start: 3 steps

  1. Choose your access point.
  2. Create your wallet.
  3. Back up your wallet.
Target time: about 5 minutes.

Step 1: Choose your access point

  • Global users: onecurrency.net
  • African users: onecurrency.africa
Both versions are the same wallet. Pick the fastest for your region.

Step 2: Create your wallet (about 60 seconds)

  1. Click "Create New Wallet".
  2. Choose 12 words (recommended) or 24 words (extra security).
  3. Set a wallet passphrase to encrypt your seed on this device.
  4. Write down your recovery words in order.
  5. Confirm the words and finish setup.
Never share your recovery words. OneCurrency will never ask for them.

Backups & Recovery

Protect your seed phrase and store encrypted backups securely.

Step 3: Backup your wallet (critical)

  1. Open the "Backup" tab.
  2. Export an encrypted backup.
  3. Save the JSON and the QR code.
  4. Store copies in safe places (email to yourself, cloud drive, USB, or password manager).
Your backup is encrypted with your password.

Restore on a new device

  1. Choose "Restore" on the new device.
  2. Enter your seed phrase or import the encrypted backup.
  3. Use the same backup password if you import JSON or QR.
  4. Confirm your balance before wiping the old device.

Fees & Pricing

Know what you pay before you send.

How fees work

  • Receiving is free. The sender pays the network fee.
  • Sending includes the fixed platform fee of 3,337 sats (0.00003337 BTC) plus the Bitcoin network fee.
  • Higher network fees usually confirm faster.

How to receive Bitcoin

  1. Open "Receive".
  2. Copy your address or show the QR code.
  3. Share it with the sender.
OneCurrency creates new addresses for each payment for privacy.

How to send Bitcoin

  1. Open "Send".
  2. Paste the recipient address.
  3. Enter the amount in BTC or sats.
  4. Review the fee estimate and confirm.
Low fee = slower confirmation. High fee = faster confirmation.

Privacy & Trust

Keep your keys private and avoid phishing.

Privacy basics

  • Your private keys stay on your device.
  • Bitcoin transactions are public on the blockchain.
  • Always verify the official domain before entering anything sensitive.

Safety & Dont Lose Money

Simple habits that prevent irreversible mistakes.

Security best practices

Do

  • Write down your recovery words.
  • Store encrypted backups in multiple places.
  • Use a strong wallet passphrase.
  • Verify addresses before sending.
  • Start with small amounts.

Security best practices

Do not

  • Share your recovery words with anyone.
  • Store recovery words in plain text.
  • Take photos of your recovery words.
  • Reuse the same address for every payment.
  • Skip the backup step.

Faucet

Claim free testnet coins (tBTC) and understand faucet limits.

Claim free testnet coins

  1. Switch the wallet to testnet mode and open the Faucet tab.
  2. Copy your testnet receiving address from the "Receive" tab.
  3. Open the public testnet faucet linked in the Faucet tab, paste your testnet address, and claim tBTC.
The faucet is testnet only. Testnet coins have no monetary value and are for practice only.
