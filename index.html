OneCurrency Bitcoin Wallet
Self-custody wallet with secure backups and a built-in faucet.
OneCurrency is a self-custody Bitcoin wallet with secure seed phrase backups and a built-in faucet. Send and receive BTC with transparent fees while keeping keys on your device.
  • Non-custodial: you control the keys.
  • Encrypted JSON and QR backups.
  • Built-in faucet for free sats.
